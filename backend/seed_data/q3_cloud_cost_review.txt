Q3 Cloud Cost Review — Internal Notes

Total monthly cloud spend for Q3 was $18,400, up 12% from Q2. The largest
contributor to the increase was the reporting-batch-worker EC2 instance,
which ran at an average utilization of 8% for the full month but was never
scaled down or scheduled to stop outside business hours. This single
instance accounted for roughly $410 of unnecessary spend.

The second largest inefficiency was the orders-db-primary RDS instance,
which was provisioned as a db.r5.2xlarge but rarely exceeded 20% CPU
utilization. Downsizing to a db.r5.large with storage autoscaling enabled
is estimated to save approximately $520 per month with no expected impact
on application performance, since peak query load remains well within the
smaller instance's headroom.

Combined, terminating or scheduling the idle batch worker and downsizing
the database instance would reduce monthly cloud spend by an estimated
$930, or roughly $11,160 annually, without any architectural changes to
the applications themselves.
